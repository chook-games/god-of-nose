# How to Play — God of Nose: Sneeze and Thunder 🤧⚡

## 🎯 Objective

You are **Babis**, a simple Stickman with terrible allergies. Your mission is to **sneeze** on everyday objects until they break! Start small (paperclips, cups) and work your way up to destroying cars with a single "Achoo!"

---

## 👆 Basic Controls

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Sneeze / Attack** | Tap the big red 👃 SNEEZE! button |
| **Switch target** | Use `<` and `>` buttons at the bottom |
| **Go back to menu** | Tap `←` button in the top-right |

---

## ⚡ Stamina — Your Energy Bar

Every sneeze costs **Stamina**. Watch the blue bar at the bottom!

- **Max Stamina:** 50 (can be upgraded later)
- **Cost per sneeze:** 2 stamina
- **Recharge:** 8 stamina per second (automatic)

### When Stamina runs out:
- The bar turns red
- "😮‍💥 Recharging..." appears
- Wait a few seconds for it to refill
- You can still tap, but Babis won't sneeze until stamina recovers

**Tip:** Don't spam taps! Let stamina recharge between bursts for maximum efficiency.

---

## 💥 Combat System

1. **Tap the 👃 SNEEZE! button** → Babis sneezes forward
2. The **target item** shakes and takes damage
3. The **HP bar** shrinks and changes color:
   - 🟢 **Green** = Full health
   - 🟡 **Yellow** = Half health
   - 🔴 **Red** = Almost destroyed
4. When HP reaches **0** → **Item destroyed!** 🎉

### Critical Hits (💥 CRIT!)
- **5% chance** per sneeze
- Deals **2x damage**
- Golden flash and "CRIT!" text appear

## 🏠 Zones & Progression

There are **5 zones** to conquer. Each zone has normal items and a final **Boss**.

### Zone 1: 🏠 Home
📎 Paperclip → ☕ Cup → 🕐 Clock → 🪑 Chair → **🗄️ WARDROBE (Boss)**

### Zone 2: ☕ Coffee Shop
🥤 Straw → 🥛 Glass → 🍯 Sugar Bowl → **☕ ESPRESSO MACHINE (Boss)**

### Zone 3: 🛒 Mini Market
🔥 Matches → 🥫 Can → 🍾 Bottle → **🧊 FRIDGE (Boss)**

### Zone 4: 🏢 Office
📌 Stapler → 🖨️ Photocopier → **💼 BOSS DESK (Boss)**

### Zone 5: 🚗 Taverna & Street
🍽️ Plate → 🪑 Table → 🏍️ Motorcycle → **🚗 CAR (Boss)**

### How to advance:
1. Destroy all items in the current zone
2. Defeat the **Boss** (has more HP, special phases, and gives Tissues)
3. Automatically advance to the next zone!

You can also use **`<` / `>` buttons** to switch between items freely (even go back to easier ones to farm Coffee Drops).

## 👑 Boss Battles — Multi-Phase Mechanics

Each zone has a **Boss** with unique attack phases. When you encounter a boss, a **"BOSS FIGHT!"** message appears and the battle begins!

### Boss Phase System
Bosses have multiple phases that activate as their HP decreases. Each phase has a different attack pattern:

| Phase Type | Effect | Visual |
|------------|--------|--------|
| 🛡️ **Shield** | Reduces incoming damage (shown as %) | Blue "SHIELDED" text |
| 💧 **Stamina Drain** | Drains your stamina over time | Red attack message |
| 🔥 **Enrage** | Heavy stamina drain, fast attacks | Red "Enraged!" message |
| 🔄 **Self Heal** | Boss heals itself periodically | Orange heal message |
| 🛡️ **Damage Reflect** | Sneeze damage reflected as stamina loss | Red "REFLECTING!" text |

### Phase Indicators
- **Gold dots** at the bottom show boss phases (● = active, ● = upcoming)
- **Phase transition messages** appear in orange when boss enters a new phase
- **Attack messages** appear in red when the boss attacks

### Boss List & Phases

| Boss | Zone | Phases |
|------|------|--------|
| 🗄️ **WARDROBE** | 🏠 Home | Phase 1: Stamina Drain → Phase 2: Damage Reflect |
| ☕ **ESPRESSO MACHINE** | ☕ Coffee Shop | Phase 1: Self Heal → Phase 2: Enrage |
| 🧊 **FRIDGE** | 🛒 Mini Market | Phase 1: Shield → Phase 2: Freeze Drain → Phase 3: Blizzard |
| 💼 **BOSS DESK** | 🏢 Office | Phase 1: Paperwork Shield → Phase 2: Filing Heal → Phase 3: Angry Boss |
| 🚗 **CAR** | 🚗 Taverna | Phase 1: Armored → Phase 2: Exhaust Fumes → Phase 3: Engine Overheat → Phase 4: Self-Repair |

### Boss Strategy Tips
- **Shielded bosses** — You need more sneeze power to break through
- **Reflecting bosses** — Be careful! Your sneezes will cost extra stamina
- **Healing bosses** — Attack fast before they recover HP
- **Enraged bosses** — They drain stamina quickly, let it recharge between bursts

---

## 🚀 Strategy Tips

1. **Farm easy items** for Coffee Drops before attempting Bosses
2. **Let stamina recharge** between bursts — don't waste taps
3. **Critical hits** are random but can turn the tide against tough items
4. **Upgrade Sneeze Power** first — it helps against shielded bosses
5. **Upgrade Max Stamina** — more stamina = more sneezes before recharging
6. **Watch for boss phases** — adapt your strategy when the boss changes attack patterns
